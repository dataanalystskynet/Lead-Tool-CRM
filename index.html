🔗 Google Sheet connection

Link of the sheet where the website form saves leads
Open sheet ↗
To use live sync, set sheet sharing once:
  1. Open the Google Sheet and click Share (top right)
  2. "General access" → Anyone with the link → role Viewer → Done
  3. Come back here and click ⟳ Sync Google Sheet
Keep the sheet columns the same: Timestamp, Source, Name, Mobile, Email, Details. If sync fails, the app shows the last saved data.
